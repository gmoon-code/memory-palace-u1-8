## Important scientific corrections made in the canonical layer

- Nuclear pores regulate exchange **between nucleus and cytoplasm**, not entry/exit from the whole cell. DNA generally remains in the nucleus; mRNA is exported.
- Free ribosome products are not restricted to the cytosol; some are imported post-translationally into the nucleus, mitochondria, chloroplasts, and peroxisomes.
- Peroxisomes are not lysosome-like digestive sacs; they are oxidative metabolic compartments with distinct enzymes.
- Modern mitochondria and chloroplasts are not autonomous free-living cells even though their bacterial ancestry is supported by genomes, ribosomes, division patterns, and membrane features.
- Cristae are folds of the mitochondrial inner membrane; the inner membrane defines matrix versus intermembrane space.
- Light reactions occur in **thylakoid membranes**; grana are stacks of thylakoids.
- Integral membrane protein is broader than transmembrane protein.
- Plant cell walls provide mechanical support and resist osmotic expansion; the plasma membrane remains the primary selective transport barrier.
- Osmosis is governed by **total water potential** when pressure and solute components differ.
- Endocytic vesicles form by plasma-membrane invagination and scission; they are not pre-existing vesicles that “fuse from” the membrane.
- Hypertonicity causes water loss and shrinkage/plasmolysis; cell death is not an automatic outcome.
