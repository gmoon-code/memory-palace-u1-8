# Unit 3 F1 Review Flags

All 30 flags are resolved at the canonical-science layer. Source wording remains preserved.

## RF-U3-001 - High - SCIENCE
**Source**: PPT-S018
**Source claim**: “Organisms obtain energy by breaking the bond between the 2nd and 3rd phosphate.”
**Why flagged**: Breaking a chemical bond requires energy. ATP hydrolysis is exergonic because products are at lower free energy than ATP plus water.
**Resolution**: Teach ATP hydrolysis as an overall free-energy-lowering reaction. Preserve slide 19’s correction that usable free energy does not come from bond breaking itself.
**Status**: RESOLVED

## RF-U3-008 - Medium - SCIENCE

## RF-U3-020 - Medium - SCIENCE
**Source**: PPT-S108
**Source claim**: “If oxygen is present, the pyruvate enters the mitochondria.”
**Why flagged**: Oxygen is not the direct signal that transports pyruvate into mitochondria. In standard aerobic eukaryotic metabolism, pyruvate is transported into mitochondria and oxidized when respiratory metabolism is proceeding.
**Resolution**: Qualify the relationship and avoid presenting O2 as the pyruvate transporter trigger.
