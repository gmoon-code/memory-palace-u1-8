# Unit 4 F1 Review Flags

All 39 flags are resolved at the canonical-science layer. Source wording remains preserved.

## RF-U4-001 — HIGH — SCIENCE
**Source slides**: PPT-S004
**Source claim / issue**: The slide says dissolved signaling substances and other cytoplasmic material can pass “freely” through gap junctions/plasmodesmata.
**Resolution**: Intercellular junctions permit passage of selected small molecules/ions or cytoplasmic continuity, but transport is constrained and can be regulated; do not encode unrestricted passage.
**Status**: RESOLVED

## RF-U4-002 — MEDIUM — SCIENCE
**Source slides**: PPT-S007
**Source claim / issue**: Paracrine signaling is defined as acting on an “adjacent” cell.
**Resolution**: Paracrine signals act locally on nearby target cells; direct membrane adjacency is not required.
**Status**: RESOLVED

## RF-U4-003 — MEDIUM — SCIENCE
**Source slides**: PPT-S009
**Source claim / issue**: Plant hormones are presented as generally traveling in xylem/phloem or through air.
**Resolution**: Plant hormones can move through tissues/vascular routes; volatile ethylene can diffuse through air. Airborne travel is a special case, not a universal plant-hormone route.
**Status**: RESOLVED

**Resolution**: Receptors have ligand-binding regions and produce intracellular effects, but not every receptor signals by handing a signal to another protein; ligand-gated channels and intracellular receptors work differently.
**Status**: RESOLVED

**Source slides**: PPT-S058

## RF-U4-022 — HIGH — SCIENCE
**Source slides**: PPT-S058
**Source claim / issue**: Mitosis is stated to result in two identical diploid daughter cells.
**Resolution**: Mitosis is nuclear division that segregates duplicated chromosomes. Mitosis plus cytokinesis usually yields two daughter cells with equivalent chromosome sets to the parent nucleus, barring errors; daughter cells are not necessarily diploid if the parent cell was haploid.
**Status**: RESOLVED
