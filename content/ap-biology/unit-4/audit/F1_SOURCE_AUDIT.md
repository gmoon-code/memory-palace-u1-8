## High-priority scientific corrections

- Direct-contact junctions do not permit unrestricted movement of arbitrary cytoplasmic material.
- Paracrine signaling targets nearby cells and does not require literal adjacency.
- The ligand carries the extracellular message; the ligand itself is usually not what is physically relayed through an intracellular transduction cascade.
- Phosphatases remove phosphate groups, but dephosphorylation does not universally mean pathway shutdown.
- GPCRs and ligand-gated ion channels are important membrane-receptor examples, not the only two membrane-receptor categories.
- GTP functions as part of the G-protein molecular switch; it should not be taught mainly as an ATP-like energy source.
- Chromosomes exist throughout the cell cycle; replication creates sister chromatids and mitosis changes chromosome condensation.
- Gametes are products of meiosis; mature gametes do not “divide by meiosis.”
- Mitosis does not universally produce diploid cells; chromosome-set equivalence depends on the parent nucleus.
- Growth factors are signaling molecules and should not be universally defined as hormones.
- Cancer does not have a universal “60 mutations” threshold; cancer-cell immortality is not literal for every cell; benign tumors are not “cancerous yet.”
- Tobacco-smoke carcinogens, not nicotine itself, are the main mutagenic cancer-risk mechanism taught here.
