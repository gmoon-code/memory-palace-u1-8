## RF-U6-001 · HISTORY · MEDIUM
Slides: 2

**Source issue**  The deck calls Franklin’s DNA work “X-Ray Crystallography” and reduces the structural evidence to a regular/repetitive pattern.

**Locked resolution**  Preserve the classroom wording in the raw ledger. Canonically describe Franklin’s X-ray diffraction work as providing critical evidence for DNA’s helical structure and dimensions; do not imply she merely supplied a picture used by others.

## RF-U6-003 · SCIENCE · HIGH
Slides: 16

**Source issue**  A quick-review item asks what bond “holds together nucleotides” and answers hydrogen bonds.

**Locked resolution**  Distinguish phosphodiester bonds within each nucleic-acid backbone from hydrogen bonds between complementary bases across paired strands/regions.
