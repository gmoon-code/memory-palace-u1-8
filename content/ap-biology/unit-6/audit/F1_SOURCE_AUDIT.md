## High-priority repairs locked in F1

- Distinguishes hydrogen bonds between paired bases from phosphodiester bonds in a strand.
- Corrects prokaryotic ribosomal subunits to **30S + 50S**, not 30S + 40S.
- Corrects the retrovirus mechanism so reverse-transcribed DNA can integrate into the **host genome**, not “become part of the RNA.”
- Treats the trp repressor as a **regulatory protein**, with tryptophan as its corepressor, not as an allosteric enzyme.
- Replaces “lactase” as the lac-operon product with accurate bacterial lac gene products such as **β-galactosidase** and lactose permease.
- Treats the genetic code as **nearly universal**, consistent with the current CED.
- Qualifies eukaryotic promoters: a **TATA box is not universal**, and the current CED requires regulatory sequences that may act upstream or downstream.
- Qualifies intron/exon language so exon is not treated as synonymous with amino-acid-coding sequence.
- Adds current-CED coverage missing or underdeveloped in the PPT, including **constitutive expression, enhancers and location, viral recombination, the three PCR functional stages, bacterial transformation, and conserved reproductive sources of variation**.
- Locks current AP exclusion boundaries for extra replication-enzyme names, translation factors, genetic-code memorization, named chromosome disorders, specific mutation details, and biotechnology procedures.
