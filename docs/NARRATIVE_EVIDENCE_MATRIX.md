# Narrative and Memory-Palace Evidence Matrix

**Product:** Memory Palace  
**Narrative design version:** NR-1.1  
**Purpose:** Separate direct Method of Loci evidence from adjacent evidence about imagery, action, narrative, and multimedia narration. This matrix is deliberately conservative. A finding is not treated as direct evidence for a memory-palace narration feature unless the study actually tested the Method of Loci.

## How to read the matrix

- **Tier A — Direct MoL evidence:** the study directly tests the Method of Loci / memory-palace procedure.
- **Tier B — Component evidence:** the study tests a component we may embed inside a locus, such as interactive imagery, enactment, causal narrative structure, signaling, or segmentation. Transfer to palace design is reasoned rather than directly proven.
- **Tier C — Historical/expert practice:** influential descriptions of the art of memory. These can suggest implementation techniques but are not treated as experimental proof.

The strongest product rules are those supported by Tier A and compatible with Tier B. Tier C is used only when it does not conflict with the experimental literature or with the scientific-safety requirements of the app.

## What we learned from “successful narration” examples

A critical result of this review is that the research literature rarely publishes and experimentally compares complete word-for-word memory-palace narrations. Therefore there is **no scientifically validated canonical script to copy**. Copying a famous memory athlete’s prose would also confuse expert tradition with causal evidence.

The defensible pattern emerges by combining the parts that *have* been studied:

1. **Route first.** The learner knows the next location without solving the story.
2. **Exact micro-anchor.** “Put your attention on the stone window sill” is stronger than “look around the laboratory.”
3. **One interactive event.** The cue and target relation physically interact.
4. **Only diagnostic distinctiveness.** One striking feature can do useful work; irrelevant spectacle is removed.
5. **Local causal coherence.** The action has a clear result or meaning. Do not build a 30-scene novel whose transitions become a second memory task.
6. **Explicit scientific translation.** The strange scene is immediately translated back into the canonical term and teacher-approved relation during encoding.
7. **Retrieval pause in review.** Cue first, silence, then confirmation.
8. **Move.** End the locus cleanly so the spatial route remains legible.

### Strong product example

> Put your attention on the **stone window sill**. A HIPPO drops a THESIS on the sill and points from a tentative explanation to a **testable prediction**. [retrieval pause] **Hypothesis.** A scientific hypothesis is a tentative explanation that leads to testable predictions. Leave the sill and move to the two Hypothesis Doors.

Why it is defensible:
- stable location;
- explicit micro-anchor;
- sound cue carries the name;
- interactive action carries the scientific meaning;
- local causal/relational coherence;
- no irrelevant world-building;
- exact term and canonical translation;
- clean spatial exit.

### Rejected product example

> You enter a beautiful neon laboratory filled with purple smoke. A hippo in sunglasses dances to loud music, knocks over a table, eats a sandwich, throws confetti, and finally writes something on a chalkboard. This reminds you of hypothesis.

The second passage is vivid, but almost all of its memorability is **unallocated**. The smoke, music, sunglasses, sandwich, table crash, and confetti can all be recalled without recovering the term *hypothesis* or its meaning. That is exactly the failure mode the NR-1.1 design is intended to prevent.

## Product rule produced by the evidence review

**ZONE → MICRO-ANCHOR → ONE DIAGNOSTIC ACTION → RETRIEVAL PAUSE → EXACT TERM → CANONICAL TRANSLATION → MOVE**

During **initial encoding**, the exact term is revealed immediately after the cue is constructed so the learner cannot encode the mnemonic without the scientific label. During **review**, the pause precedes the answer so the narration becomes retrieval practice. During **cue fading**, the keyword and then the palace are removed altogether.

This grammar is a product synthesis, **not a claim that one experiment tested this exact seven-part sentence structure**. Its components were selected because they are jointly compatible with the strongest direct MoL evidence and the adjacent experimental evidence above.
