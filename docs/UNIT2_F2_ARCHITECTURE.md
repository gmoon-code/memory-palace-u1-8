### U2-J1 — Cell Operations Complex

**Focus.** Cell identity, nucleus, ribosomes, endomembrane flow, recycling/storage, and cytoskeleton

**Why this setting works.** A coherent operations complex supports physical movement from information control to protein production, processing, recycling, storage, and internal structural support.

**Scale.** 13 loci covering 38 canonical records.
