## Character rule

Dr. Nia Park remains a guide. Scientific structures carry the mnemonic burden through their visible geometry and behavior. No additional fantasy character is introduced merely to represent vocabulary.

## Memorability rule

The learner should leave with two incompatible-looking internal maps that are difficult to confuse:

**Mitochondrion**  
outer membrane → intermembrane space → folded inner membrane/cristae → matrix

**Chloroplast**  
double envelope → thylakoid membranes → grana → stroma, with chlorophyll in thylakoid membranes

The story therefore makes distinctions through spatial organization and causal action, not through arbitrary decorative mnemonics.
