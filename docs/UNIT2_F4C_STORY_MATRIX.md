| Scene | Physical location | Central problem | Science made visible | Transition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cell Size Cube Gallery | Side-by-side cube and sphere modeling table | A giant cell has an oxygen-starved center even though it has more total surface area | SA:V, membrane area requirement, size limitation, volume scaling, cube and sphere formulas | The model cannot be shrunk, so one flat membrane section moves to the fold deck |

## Stable continuity object

The glowing exchange meter begins green for the small models, turns red as the giant model's interior becomes underserved, returns toward green after folding increases surface area, and then becomes a thermal-comparison display on the organism terrace.

## Spatial continuity

The learner begins indoors at the model table, moves one platform forward to alter surface geometry, then exits directly onto the terrace. Each move is caused by the unresolved scientific problem from the preceding scene.
