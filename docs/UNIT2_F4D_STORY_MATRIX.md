## Continuous route

The same traveler tray stays with the learner throughout the terminal. The route begins by constructing the membrane, then reveals its movement and temperature behavior, places its proteins and recognition markers, tests permeability using the traveler tray, sorts the travelers into mechanistic classes, and ends by distinguishing the plant cell wall from the plasma membrane while opening a plasmodesma to the neighboring cell.

The final concentration alarm leads directly to Journey 5, **Gradient Transit Hub**.
