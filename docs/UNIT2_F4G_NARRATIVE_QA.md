## Endosymbiosis safeguards

The F4G narrative explicitly prevents several common distortions.

- Prokaryotic cells are not portrayed as empty or spatially unorganized merely because they lack typical eukaryotic membrane-bound organelles.
- The endosymbiosis theater is identified as a historical reconstruction, not direct observation of the ancient event.
- Engulfment is followed by persistence and long-term integration rather than an instantaneous transformation into a modern organelle.
- No single evidence line is presented as independently proving endosymbiosis.
- Circular organelle DNA, bacterial-like division, bacterial-like ribosomal features, and membrane evidence are treated as converging observations.
- Modern mitochondria and chloroplasts are described as deeply host-integrated organelles and not generally capable of independent free-living existence.
