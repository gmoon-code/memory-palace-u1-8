# Unit 3 F4A Story Matrix

## Journey 1 — Enzyme Catalysis Workshop

**Story title** — *The Reaction That Would Not Start*

**Continuity object** — one amber substrate followed from free reactant to active-site binding, conversion, product release, and enzyme reset.

| Scene | Fixed left anchor | Fixed center anchor | Fixed right anchor | Main visible event | Records | Recall |
|---|---|---|---|---|---:|---|
| Catalyst Energy Ramp | Uncatalyzed energy hill | Enzyme-catalyzed lower barrier | Product-side exit | Same reaction is run over two energy profiles; catalysis lowers the activation-energy barrier while endpoints remain fixed | 4 | Yes |
| Active-Site Dock | Free-substrate approach lane | Active-site docking pocket | Enzyme-substrate complex display | Decoys fail, the compatible substrate binds, and induced fit adjusts the active site before the bound complex is frozen | 5 | Yes |
| Catalytic Turnover Bench | Fresh substrate queue | Catalytic turnover bench | Product release and enzyme reset | Products leave and the same enzyme immediately accepts another substrate; enzyme-name suffix examples are qualified | 2 | No |

## Causal route

`activation-energy problem → molecular recognition problem → catalyst-reuse problem → enzyme-regulation controls`

The next journey begins only after the learner has a working model of what an enzyme does. That prevents temperature, pH, inhibitors, allostery, cooperativity, and feedback inhibition from becoming an isolated vocabulary sequence.
