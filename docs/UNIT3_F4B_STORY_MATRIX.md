## Route continuity

The Enzyme Regulation Control Wing is an eight-station route. Enter at the stability chamber, follow the same transparent enzyme through the saturation track and helper assembly bench, then move through active-site competition, allosteric control, cooperative binding, pathway feedback, and the final irreversible-inhibition recovery bay. Left, center, and right anchors remain fixed within every station.

## Ending diagnostic model

The control wing stabilizes only after every rate change is tied to a distinct physical cause. Environmental stress, saturation, helper requirements, active-site competition, allosteric regulation, cooperative subunits, feedback inhibition, and irreversible inactivation can now be diagnosed from what happens to the enzyme or pathway.
