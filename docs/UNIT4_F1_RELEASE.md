## Source policy

## Important scientific corrections locked in F1

F1 prevents mnemonic prose from encoding several source-level oversimplifications. These include unrestricted transport through intercellular junctions, paracrine signaling requiring literal adjacency, ligand being the molecule physically relayed through the intracellular cascade, phosphatase activity universally shutting a pathway off, phosphorylation universally activating a protein, GPCRs and ion channels being the only membrane receptor types, GTP being taught mainly as ATP-like fuel, mature gametes dividing by meiosis, mitosis universally producing diploid cells, growth factors being universally hormones, and oversimplified cancer claims.

The raw source wording remains preserved in the slide ledger so the audit trail is lossless. Corrected canonical statements are what later Memory Objects and stories must use.
