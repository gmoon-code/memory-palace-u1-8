## Why 51 loci

F2 deliberately does not create one locus per canonical record. Related scientific ideas share one location when the same physical action can teach them together. This reduces visible learning burden while preserving every locked fact. Examples include ligand, receptor, specificity, and receptor activation at one reception checkpoint; chromosome, sister chromatid, centromere, and kinetochore at one chromosome-anatomy workbench; and cyclin, CDK, and cyclin–CDK complex at one regulatory rack.
