## Narrative standard

Journey 7 uses one persistent model-cell security file to connect general checkpoints, G1/G2/spindle decisions, arrest, apoptosis, cyclin–CDK regulation, extracellular proliferation cues, cancer-control failure, tumor progression, metastasis, and carcinogen risk. The story keeps checkpoint, arrest, and apoptosis distinct; avoids ranking G1 as universally the single most important checkpoint; treats DNA-damage outcomes as context-dependent; prevents specific cyclin/CDK pair catalogs from becoming AP requirements; keeps growth factors from being universally classified as hormones; defines cancer as accumulated dysregulation without a fixed mutation threshold; separates tumor, benign tumor, malignant tumor, and metastasis; and ties UV/tobacco risk to DNA damage without unsupported prevention claims.
