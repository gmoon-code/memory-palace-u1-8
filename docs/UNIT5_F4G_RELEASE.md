## Scientific continuity

- Genetic linkage remains same-chromosome inheritance tendency, not a permanent bond.
- Crossing over between nonsister chromatids creates recombinant marker combinations only when the exchange changes the tracked interval.
- Parental and recombinant types are classified against the known starting arrangement before frequency arithmetic.
- Recombination frequency remains recombinant outcomes divided by total scored outcomes and is interpreted as genetic evidence.
- For relatively short intervals, about 1 percent recombination corresponds to about 1 cM.
- Centimorgans remain genetic-map units and are not direct physical base-pair distances.
- Multiple crossovers can make observed recombination frequency underestimate longer genetic distances.
- The observed two-locus recombination fraction approaches a 50 percent ceiling and cannot by itself distinguish different chromosomes from very distant loci on the same chromosome.
