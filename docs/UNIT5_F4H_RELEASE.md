## Scientific continuity

- Non-nuclear inheritance is tied to mitochondrial and chloroplast genomes, not to traits without DNA.
- Animal mitochondrial inheritance retains usually and typically maternal qualification.
- Plant organelle inheritance retains often or predominantly maternal wording with paternal and biparental possibilities visible.
- Genetic disorder and pathogenic variant remain broader evidence-based concepts, while Tay-Sachs and sickle cell disease remain contextual examples.
- Chi-square goodness-of-fit begins with a defined null model and categorical counts.
- Observed counts remain recorded data, while expected counts remain null-model predictions.
- Category contributions retain the form (O−E)²/E and are summed into χ².
- Degrees of freedom are number of categories minus one only in the specified AP fixed-proportion goodness-of-fit context.
- Alpha remains a significance threshold, not the probability the null is true.
- Conclusions use reject or fail to reject the null. No accept-null conclusion is allowed.
