## Scope guards

Five scope guards remain teacher/support metadata and cannot become a permanent palace target, exact-name Review target, or Challenge Lab requirement.

- `U5-K-148` protects the AP boundary around detailed organism-specific sexual life cycles.
- `U5-K-149` protects chromosome-complement terminology and prevents inference of gender or reduction of sex development to X/Y chromosomes.
- `U5-K-150` keeps 3:1 and 9:3:3:1 as conditional predictions.
- `U5-K-151` keeps named human disorders illustrative.
- `U5-K-152` enforces fail-to-reject language and correct interpretation of the significance threshold.
