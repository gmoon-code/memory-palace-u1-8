## Narrative continuity

Journey 4 keeps one tracked eukaryotic gene sequence sealed while its promoter-linked expression meter and chromatin-state strip change around it. The route therefore gives the learner a stable reference for distinguishing regulation from mutation.

The trp and lac operons are separately labeled bacterial comparison systems. They never substitute for the tracked eukaryotic gene. This allows repressible and inducible operon logic to be learned without implying that eukaryotic regulation uses the same local operon architecture.

After the bacterial comparison wing, the original eukaryotic tracked gene returns and remains visible through chromatin regulation, promoter/enhancer recruitment, negative transcriptional regulation, RNA-level regulation, expression-profile changes, cell specialization, and developmental control.

## Scientific safeguards carried into prose

- Regulatory DNA and regulatory proteins remain distinct entities.
- Constitutive and inducible expression are compared through output behavior without implying every gene belongs permanently to one category.
- Promoter, operator, structural genes, and repressor protein remain physically distinct in the operon model.
- The trp repressor remains a regulatory protein. Tryptophan acts as a corepressor that enables operator binding.
- The lac operon remains an inducible bacterial system in which allolactose reduces repressor binding.
- lacZ is linked with β-galactosidase and lacY with lactose permease. The bacterial operon is not reduced to human intestinal lactase.
- Eukaryotic regulation remains multilayered across chromatin/transcription, RNA processing/stability, translation, and protein-level control.
- Histone acetylation and DNA methylation retain context-dependent wording and are not taught as universal commands.
- Epigenetic regulation remains distinct from a DNA-sequence mutation.
- Some epigenetic states can persist through cell division, while transgenerational persistence remains context dependent.
- Promoter and enhancer/control elements remain DNA sequences. Activators and repressors remain proteins.
- Enhancer and promoter effects can occur from positions upstream or downstream of the transcription start site.
- A TATA box remains one possible promoter element rather than the universal name for eukaryotic promoters.
- Negative transcriptional regulation is diagnosed through reduced transcription from a regulatory interaction while the tracked gene sequence remains fixed.
- Alternative splicing and small-RNA regulation remain separate RNA-level mechanisms.
- microRNA and siRNA are introduced as named small-RNA categories without requiring unsupported mechanistic over-detail.
- Gene-expression profiles and gene-product amount/function are both connected to phenotype.
- Differential gene expression produces specialized outputs in cells that can share the same genome.
- Cell differentiation remains distinct from morphogenesis.
- Sequential developmental gene expression, cytoplasmic determinants, induction, pattern formation/homeotic genes, morphogenesis, and apoptosis remain separate mechanisms or developmental concepts.
