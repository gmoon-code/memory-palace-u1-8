## Narrative continuity

Journey 5 keeps one blue reference DNA/chromosome state on Sora's copper ledger while each amber case changes one diagnostic feature. Mutation mechanism is identified before molecular consequence, phenotype, environmental context, inheritance, or natural-selection interpretation is added.

The route scales deliberately from nucleotide-level changes to reading-frame changes, chromosome-number errors, chromosome-structure changes, horizontal gene transfer, mobile DNA, and viral recombination. That physical scale change prevents every form of genetic variation from collapsing into the generic idea of a harmful mutation.

## Scientific safeguards carried into prose

- Mutation consequence is traced through molecular product/function before phenotype is judged.
- Beneficial, detrimental, and neutral effects retain environmental and molecular context.
- Natural selection is linked only to heritable phenotypic variation affecting reproductive success.
- Point substitution is kept distinct from silent, missense, and nonsense outcomes.
- Nonsense mutation is identified by a premature stop codon.
- Frameshift is limited to coding-region insertions/deletions whose length is not a multiple of three.
- An insertion/deletion that preserves the downstream reading frame is not called a frameshift merely because sequence length changed.
- Random mutation sources include replication/repair errors and mutagenic factors such as radiation or reactive chemicals.
- Mutation as the source of novel sequence variants remains distinct from reproductive reshuffling through crossing over, assortment, and fertilization.
- Nondisjunction is kept distinct from chromosome-structure rearrangement.
- Aneuploidy remains gain/loss of individual chromosomes and is not equated with whole-set polyploidy.
- Trisomy 21 is used only as a contextual aneuploidy example.
- Chromosomal deletion, duplication, inversion, and translocation remain visually and mechanistically distinct.
- Transformation uses environmental DNA uptake, transduction uses virus-mediated transfer, and conjugation uses direct cell-to-cell DNA transfer.
- Horizontal gene transfer remains distinct from parent-to-offspring inheritance.
- Natural transformation remains distinct from the later biotechnology use of bacterial transformation.
- Transposition is DNA-segment movement within or between DNA molecules and is not reduced to plasmid transfer.
- Viral recombination is shown with related viruses coinfecting the same host cell.
