## What F3 adds

The briefs deliberately protect the major Unit 7 failure modes before prose writing begins. Population evolution cannot be represented as an individual changing during life. Fitness remains reproductive success in context. Drift and mutation remain distinguishable from nonrandom selection. Hardy-Weinberg observed values remain separate from null-model expectations. Phylogenetic tips do not become ancestors. Reproductive isolation remains central to speciation. Miller-Urey does not create life.
