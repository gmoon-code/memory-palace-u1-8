## Continuous story design

Journey 3 uses one expanding evidence dossier with six permanent tabs for time, morphology, molecules, conserved cellular features, contemporary change, and population diversity.

Every evidence card has an **observation field** and an **inference field**. A fossil, sequence, anatomical structure, resistance record, or population-diversity case cannot enter the dossier until the narrative shows both what was observed and what that evidence can legitimately support.

The central problem is therefore argumentative rather than decorative. The museum has abundant evidence but has collapsed every observation into one vague label. The student repairs the museum by learning the evidentiary job of each category and only then allowing independent lines to converge.

## Scientific safeguards carried into visible prose

- Fossil age evidence remains separate from ancestry evidence.
- Fossil, fossil record, dating fossils, and radiometric dating remain distinct.
- Carbon-14 is limited to relatively recent organic remains and never becomes a universal fossil clock.
- Homology is tied to shared ancestry even when present-day functions differ.
- Homologous structures remain inherited structural patterns.
- Vestigial structures are never defined as functionless and may retain reduced or modified functions.
- Embryological homology remains a developmental form of common-ancestry evidence.
- Analogous structures remain similarities from independent evolution under similar selective pressures.
- Bird and bat powered flight remains independently evolved while their tetrapod forelimb skeletons remain homologous.
- DNA and protein sequence comparison uses aligned homologous regions.
- Molecular similarity supports common ancestry and relative relatedness but does not by itself generate an exact divergence time.
- Conserved membrane-bound organelles, linear chromosomes, and intron-containing genes are reactivated as common-ancestry evidence for eukaryotes.
- Antibiotic and pesticide resistance remain population-level evolutionary responses involving heritable variants and differential reproduction.
- Pathogen evolution remains population-level and can contribute to emerging disease patterns.
- Genetic diversity changes the probability that some individuals tolerate a novel environmental pressure.
- Greater genetic diversity never implies that every variant is beneficial.
- Fitness effects remain environment dependent.
- California condor demographic recovery is kept distinct from restoration of lost genetic diversity.

## Narrative benchmark

Every scene establishes left, center, and right geography before evidence is interpreted.

The same evidence dossier travels through all nine loci. Because its tab system never changes, the learner can reconstruct both the museum route and the category of inference attached to each exhibit.

The story avoids turning the museum into a list of isolated displays. The unresolved conclusion at each locus physically generates the next question. Time evidence leads into structural evidence. Structural evidence creates the homology-versus-analogy problem. Molecular evidence then contributes an independent ancestry line. Conserved cellular features broaden the common-ancestry case, contemporary data demonstrate continuing evolution, and the final diversity case connects evolutionary variation to population resilience.
