## Continuous story design

Journey 6 uses one transparent five-slot evidence tray. The slot order is permanent.

1. geological timing
2. atmospheric and geochemical conditions
3. small organic molecules
4. replicating and catalytic RNA
5. later eukaryotic endosymbiosis

A claim-strength stamp remains visible throughout the route and distinguishes evidence, historical hypothesis, experimental result, plausible model, and later evolutionary event.

The central failure is chronological and evidentiary. The lab has placed endosymbiosis before the first cells, copied modern oxygen levels into the early atmosphere, treated a historical reducing-atmosphere model as settled reconstruction, labeled Miller-Urey products as life, and separated RNA replication from the continuity and catalysis requirements that make an RNA-world model meaningful.

## Scientific safeguards carried into visible prose

- Earth formation, later habitability, and earliest fossil evidence remain in chronological order near 4.6, 3.9, and 3.5 billion years ago.
- Earliest fossil evidence is not overidentified as definitively cyanobacterial.
- Early Earth retains little free molecular oxygen.
- Lightning, ultraviolet radiation, volcanism, and geochemical gradients remain plausible energy sources for abiotic chemistry.
- Small organic molecules can arise abiotically under multiple plausible conditions.
- Meteorite organics remain one possible contributor to the prebiotic organic inventory.
- Abiotic monomer formation remains separate from polymerization, compartmentalization, self-replication, cells, and life.
- The Oparin-Haldane reducing-atmosphere model remains explicitly historical rather than a settled modern reconstruction.
- Modern evidence qualifies the atmosphere with substantial nitrogen and carbon dioxide, little oxygen, and possible locally reducing environments.
- Miller-Urey remains an experiment showing abiotic formation of amino acids and other small organics under simulated conditions.
- Miller-Urey never becomes a demonstration of macromolecule, cell, or life creation.
- The RNA-world model requires genetic continuity through copying.
- Complementary base pairing supplies templating for RNA replication.
- Catalytic RNA and ribozymes support the plausibility of catalysis before modern genetically encoded protein catalysts.
- Endosymbiosis remains a later event involving already-living host and prokaryotic cells.
- Endosymbiosis explains the origin of mitochondria and chloroplasts in eukaryotic evolution and never the initial origin of life.
