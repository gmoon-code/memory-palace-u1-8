## High-risk wording repaired before mnemonic encoding

- Fixed action patterns are **essentially** unchangeable and **usually** carried to completion once initiated.
- Imprinting occurs during a sensitive period; “first individual encountered” is not a universal rule.
- Altruism and cooperation are interpreted through direct and inclusive fitness, avoiding selection-for-the-good-of-the-population teleology.
- Energy is captured/transformed, never created by chemosynthesis or photosynthesis.
- `dN/dt` is an absolute population-change rate, not a per-capita rate by itself.
- Exponential growth assumes a constant per-capita rate under idealized conditions; the absolute number added per interval rises as N rises.
- Carrying capacity varies with environment; logistic growth is a model and real populations need not sit exactly at K.
- r/K selection is retained as a historical life-history framework of endpoints across a continuum.
- Species diversity is separated from broader biodiversity.
- Introduced species and invasive species are not synonyms.
- Keystone status depends on the community context and is not a universal label attached to a taxon.
- Current elevated extinction rates are locked without requiring a categorical claim that a completed formal mass-extinction threshold has already been crossed.
