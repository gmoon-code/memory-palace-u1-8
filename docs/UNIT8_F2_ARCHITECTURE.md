## Why Unit 8 uses this architecture

Ecology contains several different retrieval jobs. Students must retain durable identities and mechanisms, follow directional flows and cycles, distinguish closely related ecological terms, interpret population and diversity models, and transfer those models to unfamiliar graphs or data. F2 therefore gives stable biological relationships and model structures permanent spatial anchors. Closely coupled definitions and examples are embedded at the same locus. Calculations, graph interpretation, experimental design, and novel transfer remain Challenge Lab functions. Scope guards remain non-runtime protections.

The architecture also deliberately separates mechanisms that students commonly collapse. Energy flow is not matter cycling. Population density is not dispersion. Habitat is not niche. Fundamental niche is not realized niche. Introduced is not automatically invasive. Biological magnification, eutrophication, and ocean acidification have different causal chains.

## Architecture rules locked at F2

- All 255 F1 canonical records remain unchanged; F2 assigns learning function without editing scientific content.
- Behavior loci keep proximate/ultimate, innate/learned, sign-stimulus/response, kinesis/taxis, and fitness explanations explicitly discriminable.
- Energy flows one way through ecosystems while matter cycles; no locus may imply that organisms create energy or that energy is recycled.
- Biogeochemical-cycle loci must encode reservoirs and process direction, especially the distinct nitrogen transformations.
- Population equations must keep dN/dt, N, B, D, rmax, and K distinct; equation symbols remain visually attached to their biological meanings.
- Exponential and logistic models remain idealized models with explicit assumptions; carrying capacity is environment-dependent and not permanently fixed.
- r- and K-selection remain ends of a life-history continuum, not rigid organism types.
- Community loci must keep habitat/niche, fundamental/realized niche, and interaction-sign distinctions visible.
- Simpson diversity index must use the AP equation 1 − Σ(n/N)² with n and N visibly distinguished and larger values interpreted as greater diversity for this form.
- Keystone and foundation species remain distinct and context-dependent.
- Introduced does not automatically mean invasive, and mutation is never represented as directed by environmental need.
- Biological magnification, eutrophication, ocean acidification, habitat fragmentation, and overharvesting remain distinct mechanisms rather than a generic human-impact category.
- Every permanent locus requires a scientific visual in addition to any later mnemonic or narrative imagery.
