## Narrative architecture carried into prose

Journey 1 follows one continuous black floor line through twelve fixed locations. Every scene establishes the exact room and the left, center, and right scientific anchors before the biological state changes. Dr. Mira Sen remains the recurring guide, and the same transparent stimulus-response tablet persists through the route with fixed fields for cue, responder, immediate mechanism, experience, behavioral output, and fitness or homeostasis consequence.

The story uses one institute-level problem. Twelve response-room nameplates have gone dark because the distinctions among response categories have collapsed. A room relights only after its biological mechanism can be reconstructed from visible cue, responder, response, experience, and consequence. This keeps the plot tied to causal biology rather than to decorative mnemonic characters.

## Scientific safeguards carried into prose

- Behavior remains the organism's response to an internal or external stimulus, with behavioral and physiological environmental responses kept conceptually linked but distinct.
- Proximate explanation remains mechanism and development. Ultimate explanation remains evolutionary effects and history.
- Innate and learned contributions remain separable without being treated as absolute opposites in every real behavior.
- Sign stimulus remains the triggering cue. Fixed action pattern remains the response sequence, with the Campbell qualification that the sequence is usually carried to completion once initiated.
- Migration remains the regular long-distance location change. Sun, stars, and Earth's magnetic field remain possible orientation cues rather than the definition of migration.
- Kinesis remains nondirectional change in activity or turning. Taxis remains directed whole-organism movement toward or away from a stimulus.
- Phototaxis remains whole-organism movement. Phototropism remains directional plant growth.
- A signal requires transmission from one organism to another with a receiver response. Pheromone remains one chemical signal type.
- Imprinting remains long-lasting learning formed during a sensitive period, with the effective stimulus kept species and context dependent.
- Spatial, associative, and social learning remain distinguished by the information that changes behavior.
- Polygyny and polyandry remain distinguished by which sex has multiple mates, and sexual dimorphism remains a possible outcome rather than a universal condition.
- Cooperation, altruism, kin selection, and inclusive fitness remain framed through direct and indirect reproductive consequences without conscious-intention or group-good explanations.
- Photoperiodism retains relative day and night length, with night length often the critical cue for flowering.
- The hydrangea example retains the pH-to-aluminum-availability mechanism and remains context dependent.
