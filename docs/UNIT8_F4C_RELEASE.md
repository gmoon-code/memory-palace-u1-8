## Narrative architecture carried into prose

Journey 3 follows one continuous watershed route through five fixed locations. Every scene establishes the exact room and the left, center, and right scientific anchors before matter moves. Dr. Mira Sen remains the recurring Unit 8 guide. One transparent watershed reservoir board persists through the entire route with fixed reservoir boxes and transfer-arrow slots. The tracked substance changes from the shared cycle frame to water, carbon, nitrogen, and phosphorus, while reservoir versus process never changes meaning.

The plot is built around a concrete mapping failure. The watershed has lost the arrows connecting its reservoirs. Each pathway becomes recoverable only after the learner identifies where matter can remain, identifies the process that moves it, and follows the same material into a destination reservoir. This makes the shared structure visible before each cycle introduces its own chemistry and dominant reservoirs.

## Scientific safeguards carried into prose

- Reservoirs remain places where matter can remain for a period, while transfer processes remain labels on arrows between reservoirs.
- A biogeochemical cycle is reconstructed as movement of matter among biotic and abiotic reservoirs through specific processes.
- Cycle diagrams are not treated as identical closed circles across all ecosystems. Reservoir sizes, pathways, and fluxes can vary.
- Water includes evaporation, transpiration, condensation, precipitation, runoff, infiltration, groundwater, and streams.
- Carbon exchange keeps photosynthesis, cellular respiration, decomposition, and combustion distinct while preserving carbon conservation.
- Energy accounting is kept separate from matter tracking during carbon movement.
- Nitrogen fixation, assimilation, ammonification, nitrification, and denitrification are distinguished by chemical direction and biological role.
- Microorganisms remain explicit mediators of key nitrogen transformations.
- Nitrogen chemical forms remain distinct, including atmospheric N₂, ammonium, nitrite, and nitrate.
- The phosphorus cycle remains primarily sedimentary and is not given a large atmospheric reservoir.
- Water, carbon, nitrogen, and phosphorus share a reservoir-transfer frame without borrowing each other’s defining transformations.
