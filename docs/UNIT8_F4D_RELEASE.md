## Narrative architecture carried into prose

Journey 4 follows one population, R17, through seven fixed reserve stations. The population identity, mapped boundary, cohort ledger, and population-size counter N persist while only the measurement or comparison changes. Every scene establishes left, center, and right before the scientific state changes. Dr. Mira Sen remains the recurring guide, and the R17 population census card persists as the journey continuity object.

The route is designed to make several common confusions physically impossible. Density and dispersion occupy different stations. Life tables remain tabulated demographic records, while survivorship curves remain graphical age-specific survival patterns. The population-change ledger keeps N, B, D, dN/dt, immigration, emigration, and per-capita scaling on separate displays. The final two locations connect life-history traits to energetic trade-offs and preserve r-selection and K-selection as historical ends of a continuum rather than permanent species labels.

## Scientific safeguards carried into prose

- Population identity remains the same species within the mapped population boundary.
- Population density remains number of individuals per unit area or volume.
- Dispersion remains spatial arrangement and can change while density is held constant.
- Clumped, uniform, and random dispersion are linked to possible ecological causes without treating those causes as universal.
- Demography remains the study of population vital statistics, including age structure, survival, death, and reproduction.
- A life table remains distinct from a survivorship curve.
- Type I, Type II, and Type III survivorship are interpreted from curve shape and are not rigid species categories.
- In the simplified population model, dN/dt = B − D describes absolute population change per unit time when migration is omitted.
- dN/dt is kept distinct from a per-capita rate scaled to N.
- Immigration and emigration remain explicit additions to a fuller population accounting.
- Life-history traits remain connected through energetic trade-offs without teleological adaptation language.
- r-selection and K-selection remain endpoints of a life-history continuum.
- Carrying capacity K is not represented as a fixed species constant.
