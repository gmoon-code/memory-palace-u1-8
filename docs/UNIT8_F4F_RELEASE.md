## Narrative architecture carried into prose

Journey 6 carries one shared community map through eight fixed district locations. Species A, Species B, Species C, habitat layers, resource-use bands, abundance markers, interaction signs, disturbance marks, and succession starting-condition symbols remain visible as the route advances. The story therefore preserves identity and spatial continuity while changing one ecological relationship at a time.

The route is built around distinctions that students commonly collapse. Habitat occupies the physical-environment layer while niche occupies the resource-use and conditions layer. Fundamental and realized niches remain nested potential-versus-occupied envelopes. Interaction signs describe effects on each participant and never replace the underlying mechanism. Competition moves onto a limiting-resource axis before exclusion or partitioning is considered. Consumer interactions and defenses occupy a separate corridor. Symbioses are classified from measurable effects. Succession begins only after the pre-disturbance community and starting conditions remain visible.

## Scientific safeguards carried into prose

- Community remains a set of populations of different species living close enough to interact and does not replace population or ecosystem as an ecological level.
- Habitat remains where an organism or population lives. Ecological niche remains how a species uses biotic and abiotic resources and the conditions under which it persists.
- Fundamental niche remains the potential range under the stated absence of constraining biotic interactions. Realized niche remains the occupied subset after competition, predation, and other interactions.
- Interspecific interaction signs remain summaries of effects on each species. They are not treated as mechanisms by themselves.
- Trophic cascades remain indirect network consequences that follow from linked interactions.
- Competition remains a −/− interaction over a shared limiting resource.
- Competitive exclusion remains conditional on stable conditions, effectively identical niches, and competition for the same limiting resource. The narrative does not claim that any two competitors inevitably cause extinction.
- Resource partitioning remains differentiation in resource use that reduces direct competition. Present-day partitioning is not used to invent a specific evolutionary history.
- Predation remains killing and consumption of prey. Herbivory remains consumption of plant or algal tissue.
- Cryptic coloration, Batesian mimicry, and Müllerian mimicry remain separate defense mechanisms.
- Symbiosis does not imply mutual benefit. Parasitism, mutualism, and commensalism remain distinguished by +/−, +/+, and +/0 effects.
- Facilitation remains a positive effect that need not fit a simple feeding relationship or the same close association used to define symbiosis.
- Ecological succession remains sequential community change and is not represented as a guaranteed march toward one universal endpoint.
- Organisms can change light, soil development, nutrients, moisture, and other abiotic conditions during succession, while pH and mineral effects remain context-dependent.
- Primary and secondary succession are classified from whether an established soil community and biological legacies remain, not from visual disturbance severity.
