## Narrative architecture carried into prose

Journey 8 carries one baseline landscape record through all eight response-center locations. Habitat area, focal population size, community composition, trophic structure, water chemistry, and geographic context remain available while each room changes one driver. The learner therefore sees each environmental-change mechanism as a causal modification of one continuing ecological system rather than as an isolated vocabulary list.

The route preserves the locked F3 left-center-right geometry in every scene. Variation precedes selection. Arrival precedes establishment, spread, and harmful impact. Habitat area loss remains visible separately from isolation. Recruitment is measured against removal. Persistent contaminant transfer remains separate from acid-deposition chemistry. Nutrient enrichment is traced through production, decomposition, oxygen demand, and community change. Carbon dioxide is traced through carbonate chemistry, lower pH, and reduced carbonate availability. Regional geological, meteorological, latitudinal, and disease patterns remain attached to evidence and context.

## Scientific safeguards carried into prose

- Mutation remains nonteleological. Useful mutations do not appear because organisms need them.
- Adaptation remains a population-level result of natural selection acting on heritable variation in a particular environment.
- Heterozygote advantage remains a relative-fitness relationship that depends on environmental conditions.
- Introduced species remain distinct from invasive species. Introduction alone does not justify the invasive label.
- Invasive classification requires establishment and spread with harmful ecological, economic, or health effects in the relevant context.
- Habitat loss and fragmentation remain major biodiversity pressures without being encoded as a universal single greatest threat.
- Habitat area and isolation remain distinguishable mechanisms.
- The species-area relationship remains a broad pattern with multiple contributing mechanisms.
- Overharvesting remains tied to removals exceeding population replacement over time.
- Elevated extinction rates and the current biodiversity crisis remain explicit while formal completed-mass-extinction classification stays qualified by definition and time scale.
- Biological magnification remains a trophic concentration pattern for suitable persistent substances.
- Acid deposition remains a separate chemical pathway whose effects depend on pollutant, exposure, and buffering.
- Eutrophication preserves the nutrient → production → decomposition → oxygen-demand → dissolved-oxygen decline → community-change chain.
- Ocean acidification preserves anthropogenic CO₂ dissolution, carbonate-chemistry shift, lower pH, and reduced carbonate availability without claiming identical responses for every calcifying organism.
- Geological and meteorological change remains a broad driver of habitat and ecosystem distribution.
- The latitudinal diversity gradient remains a broad multicausal pattern rather than a single-cause law.
- Disease ecology remains context-dependent across host density, immunity, species interactions, environment, and pathogen traits.
