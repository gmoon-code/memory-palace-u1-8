## Authority lock

The source roles are intentionally separate.

The College Board CED controls the organization of the real course. Units will appear as CED Units 1 through 9, and topics will follow the order in the CED unit guides.

The teacher PowerPoints do not control sequence. Their U1 through U15 labels are retained only as source identifiers. During F0B, individual ideas from a deck may move to another CED unit, may be split across several CED topics, or may be identified as prerequisite/review material. The PowerPoints control the level students are expected to understand, the amount of explanation and calculation practice expected, representative classroom examples, and the kinds of reasoning emphasized.

Zumdahl, Zumdahl, and DeCoste's `Chemistry`, 11th edition, is the chemistry-accuracy and explanatory reference. A PPT statement that requires correction or clarification will be recorded during crosswalking and resolved to chemistry consistent with the textbook. The CED still limits AP scope, so additional textbook material is not added merely because it exists in the chapter.

The scoring-guide collection supplies assessment evidence. It shows the forms of reasoning, representation, calculation, experimental analysis, graph interpretation, particulate-model interpretation, and explanation demanded across the CED units. Source questions, scoring text, and answer explanations are not copied verbatim into the Story Method course.
