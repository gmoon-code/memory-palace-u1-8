## Recorded teacher-depth gaps and partial mappings

### 1.4 Composition of Mixtures

The PPT collection teaches percent composition and purity-related calculations but does not present the CED composition-of-mixtures topic as a distinct instructional sequence. Preserve the CED framing during production.

### 2.2 Intramolecular Force and Potential Energy

Teacher slides directly teach bond order, internuclear distance, bond strength, and bond-energy calculations. They do not clearly teach the full CED potential-energy-versus-internuclear-distance curve as a dedicated concept. CED and Zumdahl must supply that representation without exceeding PPT depth.

### 7.8 Representations of Equilibrium

The equilibrium PPT teaches dynamic equilibrium, Q, K, ICE tables, and equilibrium graphs, but a direct particulate-model treatment matching CED Topic 7.8 was not located. This teacher-depth gap must remain visible before content lock.

Assessment evidence note: No textual particulate-model marker was located in APCHEM-7-SG.pdf during F0B screening. Treat the unit guide as general assessment evidence only.

### 8.10 Buffer Capacity

The acid-base PPT teaches buffers and Henderson-Hasselbalch but does not explicitly teach buffer capacity as a named quantitative or experimental topic. The supplied Unit 8 scoring guide does contain buffer-capacity assessment evidence.

Assessment evidence note: APCHEM-8-SG.pdf contains explicit buffer-capacity assessment evidence.

### 8.11 pH and Solubility

The Ksp PPT includes a limited pH/solubility prompt but does not develop the CED pH-sensitive-solubility relationship systematically. CED and Zumdahl provide the scientific basis; the student-depth target stays conservative until confirmed.

### 9.6 Free Energy of Dissolution

The solutions PPT teaches enthalpy changes in solution formation, while the advanced thermochemistry PPT teaches entropy and free energy separately. No single PPT sequence directly integrates these into the CED Free Energy of Dissolution topic.

### 9.7 Coupled Reactions

No direct teacher-PPT treatment of coupled reactions was located. Zumdahl 11e explicitly covers external energy and coupled thermodynamically unfavorable and favorable reactions, while the CED requires the topic. This gap must remain flagged through F0D.

Assessment evidence note: No direct coupled-reaction wording was located in APCHEM-9-SG.pdf during F0B screening. CED and Zumdahl remain the direct sources.
