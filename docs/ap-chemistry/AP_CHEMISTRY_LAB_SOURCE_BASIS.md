# AP Chemistry laboratory source basis

## Status

The laboratory-source blocker is cleared by explicit user directive. The project will use the AP Chemistry CED and current public College Board laboratory guidance as the laboratory source basis.

This source basis establishes the laboratory requirements and inquiry expectations for the course. It does not claim that College Board assigned a specific experiment to any individual CED topic.

## Inventoried official sources

The AP Chemistry Course and Exam Description remains the framework authority. Its laboratory expectation is carried forward as a course-level lock of at least 25 percent of instructional time in laboratory investigations, at least 16 hands-on investigations, and at least 6 guided-inquiry investigations.

The current AP Chemistry Course Audit page supplies the public curricular and resource requirements for the 2026-27 authorization cycle. It requires hands-on, inquiry-based laboratory work that supports required content and science practices. It also requires students to maintain evidence of investigations and communicate that evidence. The public page identifies `AP Chemistry Guided Inquiry Experiments: Applying the Science Practices` or other inquiry-based/student-directed activities that meet the course objectives as acceptable resources.

The public College Board hands-on-lab FAQ supplies the operational definition used for Course Audit. Hands-on work involves students manipulating, observing, exploring, and reasoning with concrete materials. Virtual laboratories can count when students interact with computer-generated objects, data, or phenomena to meet laboratory objectives. Teacher demonstrations alone do not satisfy the laboratory requirement.

## Source-boundary rule

The public guidance does not prescribe a complete teacher lab sequence or a topic-by-topic list of required experiments. The project therefore does not attribute specific procedures to College Board.

The named `AP Chemistry Guided Inquiry Experiments: Applying the Science Practices` resource is referenced by the Course Audit page but has not been ingested as a source document. No procedure from that manual may be reproduced or presented as if it had been sourced here unless the manual is later supplied or otherwise lawfully available.

## Topic mapping

F0C already classified every CED topic by experimental-reasoning priority. That existing classification now becomes the planning basis for lab-context placement.

- High experimental-reasoning topics become **core lab candidates**.
- Medium experimental-reasoning topics become **supporting lab candidates**.
- General topics remain **context-only** unless a later source-supported lab design needs them.

This is a Story Method planning classification. It is not an official College Board lab assignment.

Current counts are 18 core candidates, 25 supporting candidates, and 48 context-only topics.

## Later lab-design requirements

The later Challenge Lab plan must select at least 16 hands-on investigations, with at least 6 in guided-inquiry format, while preserving at least 25 percent laboratory instructional time across the course.

Each selected lab must have a source-supported chemistry objective, a clear relationship to one or more CED topics/science practices, student-generated evidence or data, analysis/reasoning requirements, and a student record or communication product.

Specific procedures may be original Story Method activities or independently sourced activities. They cannot be attributed to College Board unless the underlying College Board procedure is actually available as a source.

## F0D clearance

This inventoried source basis satisfies the laboratory-source clearance option recorded by the blocked F0D gate. It removes the requirement for a separate teacher-specific lab collection. It does not make AP Chemistry student-visible or teacher-editable. Those remain later release decisions.
